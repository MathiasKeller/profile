Themes

What I keep coming back to

The shrinking Swiss labour force

Already contracting. Retirements peak at the end of this decade and immigration stops closing the gap around 2030. The real question isn't headcount planning — it's what HR operating models have to become.

Suite versus best-of-breed

Companies get this wrong in both directions: buying integration they never use, or specialisation they can't operate. The feature list almost never decides it.

The partnership behind the product

How a vendor treats its partners predicts your experience of the software better than any demo. Most buyers never look at that layer.

AI in HR, minus the theatre

Where automation and matching genuinely change the work, where they quietly don't, and what data protection and the EU AI Act will actually let a Swiss employer do.
